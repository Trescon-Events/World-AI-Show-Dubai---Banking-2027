17Content rules — do not break (11 rules)

#RuleOperational & Commercial Rationale
1Event Duration: State strictly as a 1-Day Executive Decision Forum.Reflects the master execution timeline on 24 March 2027 and its pairing with Healthcare on 25 March.
2Clustering Model: Position Dubai as Day 1 of the March Dubai Cluster.Paired back-to-back with AI in Healthcare & Life Sciences, sharing venue infrastructure while maintaining distinct P&Ls.
3Partner Durations: State speaking slots strictly as 20-minute plenary keynotes or 75-minute workshop clinics.Aligns with approved commercial tier deliverables for Speaking Sponsors and Engagement Partners.
4Booth Sizes: Reference strictly as 6 sqm, 9 sqm, and 12 sqm.Standardized floor space allocation across Speaking Sponsors (12 sqm), Engagement/Industry Partners (9 sqm), and Exhibitors (6 sqm).
5Never display sponsorship pricing on the public website.Commercial inventory is strictly capped at USD 150,000; pricing lives strictly in the private sales rate card.
6"By invitation" must never appear as an unqualified claim about the whole event.Complimentary delegate passes are by invitation and qualification; paid business passes exist alongside. "By invitation only" is prohibited.
7Consent Qualifier: Delegate contact details are shared only where explicit consent is provided under UAE PDPL.Mandatory compliance requirement under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law. Never promise unconsented attendee data.
8Regulatory Delineation: Always distinguish between federal CBUAE jurisdiction and offshore DIFC DFSA jurisdiction.Never conflate onshore federal UAE banking rules with DIFC free-zone rules in published copy.
9Regulatory Patronage Guardrail: Never imply official endorsement or patronage from CBUAE, DFSA, or DIFC without signed agreements.Protects institutional neutrality and corporate liability.
10UK English throughout (programme, organise, prioritise, analyse, centre, modernisation).Maintains consistent editorial standards across the global series portfolio.
11Organiser Attribution: Always state "Organised by Trescon" (never "managed by" or "in association with").Maintains corporate entity governance and series brand ownership.
