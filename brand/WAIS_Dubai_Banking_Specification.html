01How to use this document

This is an authoritative build specification, not a visual mockup. Every line of body copy, heading, research data point, regulatory benchmark, and call to action below is cleared and verbatim — it must be used as written rather than rewritten, paraphrased, or compressed during frontend implementation.

Working in Antigravity & Web Development Workflows

Each page section below is written to be directly usable as an implementation prompt on its own:

  1. Start with Brand kit, Layout system, and Component library (sections 02–05). Establish the core tokens and styles first — eighty percent of the user interface relies on these modular components.
  2. Build the Home page next, section by section, in chronological order. It exercises the design tokens and serves as the benchmark template for all subsequent pages.
  3. Assemble the Agenda, Attend, Speakers, Partners, Exhibition, Networking, and Enquiry suites using the unified World AI Show navigation architecture (sections 07–13). Connect all conversion endpoints to HubSpot Portal ID 2953901.
Prompting note

Copy a single wireframe block into your development workflow along with its layout instructions rather than pasting entire suites. Where a block specifies an ASSET callout, build the container to the stated dimensions and leave it empty until approved creative files arrive.

02Brand kit & design direction

The visual identity incorporates the master brand guidelines established for the World AI Show platform. It projects an authoritative, high-density, luminous enterprise feel engineered for commercial bank CXOs, risk leaders, chief data officers, and regulatory stakeholders.

Tone of the design

The audience consists of commercial bank CXOs, Chief Risk Officers (CROs), Chief Compliance Officers (CCOs), Heads of AML, Heads of Fraud Risk, Chief Data & AI Officers, Enterprise Architects, and Financial Regulators. The visual aesthetic must convey audited execution, sovereign security, and fiduciary rigor rather than consumer fintech hype.

DoDeep obsidian backgrounds (#020814), glass cards with subtle borders, clean mono labels, high-contrast Cyber Lime CTA pills with dark ink, and verified regulatory evidence benchmarks.
AvoidGeneric fintech gradients, retail POS terminals, crypto/Web3 tokens, academic AGI debates, ungrounded futurism, and countdown pressure timers.

Rule — Button Ink Guardrail

Never place white text inside a Cyber Lime (#C0F43C) button or badge. White on lime yields an unreadable 1.3:1 contrast ratio. Typography inside Cyber Lime elements must strictly use Carbon Ink (#06111F !important).
